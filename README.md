# Mizuho-Internship-Projects
Displaying Powerpoints and Screenshots representing some of the work I did during my summer internship at Mizuho

All of the trade ideas are relative value ones, in which the client or the bank buys one asset and sells another simultaneously

I conducted research using PCA to come up with a trade idea on Japanese swap rates, I then created a dashboard the conducts similar analysis for major sovereign swap and bond curves

I utilised cointegration for my final trade idea which identifies bond pairs that have a historically stable relationship in their z-spread, but has a current deviation from that relationship which can allow for a mean reverting trade to be put on

Note:
I redacted some private information from the final trade idea presentation, hence why there are some black boxes in the pdf
